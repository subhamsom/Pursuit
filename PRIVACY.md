Privacy Policy — Pursuit
Pursuit does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data from its users.
The extension makes a single outbound request to api.pexels.com to fetch a background photo. This request contains no user information. The photo URL is cached in the browser's localStorage for 6 hours. This data never leaves the user's device.
No analytics, no tracking, no login, no account. The extension has no backend and no database.
